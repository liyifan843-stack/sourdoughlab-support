Sourdough Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Sourdough Lab for Mac does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or third-party SDKs.

Recipe values remain on your Mac. When you use Export CSV, the app writes a file only to the location you choose through the macOS save panel. The app does not upload that file.
